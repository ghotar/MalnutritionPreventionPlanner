Village Famine Prevention Planner

District-officer dashboard for forward planning in remote villages across Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, and West Bengal. The model simulates households, village ecology, shocks, entitlement failures, nutrition, and intervention effects. It runs weekly internally and summarizes yearly.

Version 1 uses a transparent rule-based agent simulation with Monte Carlo runs and confidence bands. The purpose is planning usefulness, not black-box prediction.

Model Structure
weekly internal simulation yearly summary state packs households + farms entitlements maternal care cooperation Monte Carlo bands
  • Human agents are tracked conceptually in exact weeks from birth and grouped inside multigenerational households.
  • Pregnancy uses weekly conception hazard by female age. Under-5 and maternal nutrition states modify outcomes.
  • Households share labor, food, debt, entitlement access, and kin support.
  • Village resilience depends on farm output, bovines, commons ecology, prices, and social cohesion.
  • The officer can deploy strong interventions early, especially long-range water, food, and maternal measures.
